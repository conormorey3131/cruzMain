About Cruz Ireland

Cruz Ireland is a community of car enthusiasts dedicated to organizing and promoting car meets across Ireland. From Cork to Dingle, Mondello to Watergrasshill, we bring together passionate car owners and admirers to celebrate automotive culture.

Our events specialize in showcasing the finest modified cars across Ireland and beyond. From iconic Japanese cars to High performance European cars, and everything in between, Cruz events always showcase the coolest cars to be found across the Emerald Isle.
